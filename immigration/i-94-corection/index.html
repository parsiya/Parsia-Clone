    Correcting I-94 records for US-Canada Border

    Note: US citizens do not show up here. US permanent residents records are also spotty.

    Entry/exit records especially via the land border are mixed up. Neither country has exist checks so it relies on the other side to report. "non-controlled Canadians" (the term is hilarious but it's actually the immigration jargon) which are basically Canadians who enter as a visitor like you via land or sea may or may not get an I-94 so their travel records are very prone to this issue.

    If you want to correct it on the US side. While this looks like it's written by AI, it's not.

    Step 1: Enter your information and passport number here: https://i94.cbp.dhs.gov/search/history-search. This will check your entry/exit records for the US side. Check it against your own travel records.

    Step 2: Figure out when you left. Write down the date. If you have anything related to it like a credit card charge in Canada very close to your departure date, gather them and convert to PDF.

    Step 3: Go to this website. https://www.cbp.gov/about/contact/ports. Click on the state where you left via. This will show you all the Ports of Entry (POE) for the state. Find the the "Field Operation Office" (last column) for departure or arrival. Note not all ports of entries in a state are served by the same field office. E.g., there are POEs in WA served by the Portland field office.

    Step 4: Now that you have the field office. Go to https://www.cbp.gov/about/contact/ports/deferred-inspection-sites and choose the field office. You should see an email for "I-94 Correction Instructions."

    Step 5: Email that address and mention your exit record is missing and ask them to fix it. Create a list like this. You want to make it as easy as possible for the officer to see the info and take action.

    • First Name: Jane
    • Last Name: Doe
    • DOB: 01/20/1990 (note the mm-dd-yyyy format).
    • Passport number: J123456
    • Date of Exit: 01/01/2020
    • Port of Exit: You can figure this out from your I-94 travel records in step 1, assuming you've always used the same port. This is typically a number in the I-94 portal.

    And then ask for the record to be corrected. Ask AI to give you a simple email if you do not want to write it.

    You will quickly get an automated response. But you probably will not get a reply saying this was fixed. Check your records in a week and typically it is fixed. If not, wait another week and email after two weeks from the start.
